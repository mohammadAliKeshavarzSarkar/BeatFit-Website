# Second Persian Coach Article Design

## Intent

Publish the supplied Persian article for coaches as the second production blog article, focused on controlling relative training intensity in group classes rather than advertising the coach dashboard directly.

## Scope

- Canonical URL: `/blog/کنترل-شدت-تمرین-در-کلاس-گروهی`.
- Keep `/blog/live-class-monitoring` as a redirect alias.
- Use `BeatFit Training Goals Discussion.png` as the hero and Resources-card image.
- Use `Coach Reviewing BeatFit Fitness Dashboard.png` inside the live-monitoring/BeatFit section.
- Keep `/beatfitImages/blog-live-class.png` unchanged on the homepage card.
- Publish the complete supplied copy with two comparison tables, the two-athlete relative-intensity chart, FAQ, seven sources, coach CTA, Persian SEO metadata, and internal links to the first article and `/coach`.

## Approaches

1. Reuse the rich blog block system and add the article as a focused content module. Chosen because it preserves the first article’s responsive tables, chart, FAQ, source, SEO, routing, and card behavior.
2. Build a one-off coach article page. Rejected because it duplicates the renderer and makes future articles inconsistent.
3. Keep the legacy short article and append supplied text as plain paragraphs. Rejected because it loses the requested tables, chart, semantic structure, internal links, and SEO quality.

## Constraints and non-goals

- Persian/RTL is canonical; there is no English article version.
- Do not replace the existing homepage card image.
- Do not redesign the blog system or other articles.
- The chart is an educational hypothetical example, not a real athlete record.
