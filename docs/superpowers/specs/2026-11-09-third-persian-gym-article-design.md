# Third Persian Gym Article Design

## Intent

Publish the supplied Persian article for gym owners as the third production blog article, explaining how group heart-rate monitoring works, what it requires, where it adds value, and how a manager can evaluate it.

## Scope

- Canonical URL: `/blog/پایش-ضربان-قلب-در-باشگاه`.
- Keep `/blog/connected-training-system` as a redirect alias.
- Use `BeatFit Group Training in Action.png` as the hero and primary Resources-card image.
- Use `Coach Reviewing BeatFit Fitness Dashboard.png` inside the BeatFit capabilities section.
- Keep `/beatfitImages/blog-club-data-flow.png` unchanged on the homepage card.
- Publish the complete supplied copy with a four-step system-flow diagram, two management tables, FAQ, seven sources, Persian SEO, links to articles one and two, and a `/demo` CTA.

## Approaches

1. Reuse the rich blog system and add a generic `process` block for the four-step flow. Chosen because the article explicitly rejects a statistical chart and the new block will remain reusable for future operational articles.
2. Render the flow as a static image. Rejected because text would be inaccessible, harder to translate, and less responsive.
3. Approximate the flow with the existing chart block. Rejected because it would misrepresent a sequence as quantitative data.

## Constraints and non-goals

- Persian/RTL is canonical; there is no English article version.
- Do not replace homepage card images.
- Do not redesign existing articles or alter their content.
- The flow diagram describes a simplified architecture, not a product-specific technical guarantee.
