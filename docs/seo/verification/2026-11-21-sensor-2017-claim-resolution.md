## Specific devices, study and statistics

Gillinov et al. (2017), PMID **28709155**, tested **50 participants** against ECG during specified aerobic exercise protocols. Polar H7 chest strap, Scosche Rhythm+ forearm optical monitor and selected wrist watches (including Apple Watch) were evaluated. The 2018 scholarly review's Table 3 reports ECG concordance `r_c` of approximately **0.99** for Polar H7, **0.92** for Apple Watch and **0.75** for Scosche Rhythm+ in that study.

**Claim verdict:** **SUPPORTED WITH DEVICE/ACTIVITY LIMITATIONS**. Apple Watch had higher reported agreement than the Scosche forearm device in the tested setting. This supports the existing cautious BeatFit sentence; it does **not** imply that all wrist watches are superior to all arm sensors. Other tests, populations, firmware and motion conditions may differ.
