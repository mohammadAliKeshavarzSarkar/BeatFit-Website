## Three prominent differentiators to validate and explain

1. **Complete class system:** Live visibility across multiple participants and device/screen/panel/app integration (claimed in BeatFit site source; actual deployed/operational behavior needs proof).
2. **Class-to-history continuity:** Real-time feedback plus recorded session history; show accurate product screenshots and interpreted sample metrics instead of general claims.
3. **Audience-specific explanations:** Gym business value, coach control, athlete progress are distinct jobs. One universal CTA/form may frustrate users.

## Content gaps are hypotheses, not measured losses

- Practical five-zone calculation workflow: sampled results include tools but BeatFit currently has a guide.
- Commercial FAQ addressing onboarding, compatible sensors and live-display workflow: competitor pages show these categories, but current BeatFit source already includes some onboarding claims; investigate coverage before adding more.
- Transparent sensor evidence and caveats: compare technology, fit, sampling artifacts and device validation rather than assert universal superiority.
- Source-visible full content and accessible internal links: previous source audit found only a temporary SEO H1+summary. **Do not claim that Google cannot render JavaScript; Google Search Central documents how it renders JS.** Confirm output before deciding on SSG/SSR.
