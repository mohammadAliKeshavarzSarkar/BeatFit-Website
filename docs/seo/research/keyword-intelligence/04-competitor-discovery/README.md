# Phase 04 — Competitor discovery

**Research date:** 2026-11-25. **Scope:** Public web discovery for Persian-speaking users in Iran; not an Iran-localized Google SERP measurement. **Status:** Initial discovery complete; market presence, demand and rankings unverified.

## Deliverables

- [Competitor register](competitors.csv): 27 distinct research entities or publisher/brand cases; nine direct monitoring benchmarks, five hardware retailers, four gym/coaching platforms, two fitness apps, four educational/tool publishers and three adjacent-intent cases.
- [Evidence register](evidence.csv): 34 observed public-page references (27 representative and seven supplementary). Each links to a stable competitor ID. Observations are paraphrases of available search excerpts; product capabilities are vendor-described, not independently tested.
- Priority P0/P1/P2 means order of Phase 05 research, not market share, SEO difficulty or measured ranking.

## Method

Sampled public web results on 2026-11-25 using the following query families: Persian sports heart-rate monitoring and live gym displays; wearable sensor, armband and chest strap retail; Iranian-facing gym and coaching software; Persian heart-rate calculators and educational content; English group heart rate platforms; brand spelling collision. Additional targeted domain queries established specific educational, retail and medical-adjacent examples. Searches were batched; no exact query-result position or individualized SERP observation was recorded.

Retain platforms when a representative public source demonstrates product overlap, vocabulary overlap, adjacent search intent, or a relevant brand-name collision. Distinguish platform origin from page language: Persian localization does not establish sales or operation inside Iran. An international benchmark is not necessarily an organic search competitor in Iran. No exhaustive competitor list is claimed.

## Findings and priorities

**Direct platforms:** Myzone, Polar Club, Prism and FitMao are the first product-positioning benchmarks. Prism and FitMao publish Persian pages; compare native terminology against translated phrases. Uptivo, SelfLoops, Spivi, FitCollab and Chileaf add useful group-monitoring, screen, hardware and engagement vocabulary.

**Hardware:** Technolife, Kiyan Darya, Garmin Store, The RunDone and MrZon offer relevant Persian commercial terminology. Retail listings can demonstrate how products are described but not prove claimed accuracy or BeatFit compatibility. Keep purchase/price searches while actual BeatFit sales model remains a gate.

**Gym/coaching:** Gymova, Dambel, Iran Badan and Oxyzone address gym/coach/athlete workflows and performance tracking. Their live heart-rate capabilities were not verified; do not describe them as feature-equivalent. ProGym and GymShow Coach provide adjacent app and reporting vocabulary.

**Education:** FitTools and Fitova offer heart-rate calculators; Varzeshcast discusses wearable selection; Axon Life covers exercise heart rate. Analyze content formats and topics, not reproduce health claims.

**Adjacent intent:** Honam Med and Dr Afshari's site demonstrate clinical/health overlap for broad phrases. A Radio Farda article dated 2020-12-17 (27 Azar 1399) about Fitbit uses both فیت‌بیت and بیت‌فیت in the same article. This is concrete historical spelling-collision evidence; it does not establish Google currently conflates the brands. Technolife and mobile.ir provide supplementary Fitbit coverage. Do not use another brand as an alias for BeatFit. A previously mentioned bfit.ir relationship remains unverified and is not classified as a competitor in this register.

## Phase 05 handoff

For each P0 record first, collect representative public pages, exact title/H1 where accessible, original Persian vocabulary, translated English concepts explicitly marked as derived, observed content format, target audience, provisional intent, source URL, retrieval date and evidence status. Use P1 and P2 records to extend coverage afterward. Do not fabricate competitors' ranking keywords, volumes, traffic, links or page metadata from snippets. Where full pages are inaccessible, mark the evidence limitation. Keep medical queries and competitor trademarks as research entities rather than unauthorized marketing claims.

## Acceptance and limits

27 unique IDs and representative URLs; 34 evidence records linked to the register; no unsupported rank or market-presence values. This establishes the initial discovery roster, not its exhaustive completeness or real Google rankings. Phase 03 direct Google surface capture is still blocked/pending. Phase 05 can proceed with page-content analysis; actual ranking and search demand measurements remain Phase 06/08/14 work.

Research only. No website copy, source code, new pages, deployment, or external outreach.
