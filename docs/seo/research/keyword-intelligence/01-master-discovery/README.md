# Phase 01 — Master keyword discovery

**Record date:** 2026-11-25. **Status:** Initial inventory complete; measured demand not verified.

The [keyword inventory](keywords.csv) contains **462 distinct exact phrases**, reconstructed from the earlier conversation's 14 keyword families and prior keyword bank, plus every keyword phrase in the existing 113-row [canonical map v4](../../2026-11-24-canonical-keyword-map-v4.csv) and 36-row [priority register](../../2026-11-24-priority-ranking-query-register.csv). Existing source rows and page associations are preserved in those original files; the new inventory records their provenance. This is a retrospective research record, not a claim of exhaustive discovery or a fresh ranking measurement. Some phrases belong to several families.

## Scope

Brand, monitoring, hardware, gyms, group training, coaching, software, sports science, performance, fitness, technology, specific sports, purchasing and calculation/question tasks.

Representative vocabulary sources: [Technolife](https://www.technolife.com/category/health-beauty/health/health-measurement/heart-rate-monitors), [Kiyan Darya](https://kiyandaria.com/cat/heart-rate-monitor-armband), [Prism](https://www.prismgym.com/fa), [FitTools](https://fittools.ir/heart-rate.html). Earlier source lists were exploratory. The original conversation wording is recorded as candidates rather than falsely attributed to Google queries.

## Rules

- Preserve exact raw text and spelling; exact-string deduplication only, not semantic merging.
- Retain ambiguous broad queries (بیت، فیت، ضربان قلب) for later intent classification.
- Never infer availability of device models, medical features, sales, training metrics or compatibility from competitor wording.
- Every row has unverified volume and rank status. No target-page or content changes are authorized.

## Acceptance

462 unique raw phrases; all 113 baseline rows and all 36 priority rows imported by phrase and provenance. Normalized grouping, complete source attribution and measured demand remain later-phase tasks.
